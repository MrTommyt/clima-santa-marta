# ADR-0001: Keep it Santa Marta only

- **Status**: Accepted
- **Date**: YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

This is a showcase of my skills as an AI/ML Engineer, this is not meant to be a fully fledged application, and hence I purposefully constrain it to something I have more knowledge of which is my city, instead of a weather system when I'm no metereologist.

## Decision

Keep all data and decisions Santa Marta focused only.

## Consequences

I know my city, and the quirks that would allow me to better design something around it, I can also more easily contact a network of people that could allow me to understand problems easier if they're constrained to the city they also most likely know instead of a general system that'd be designed to be more accurate in general or for the most amount of people possible.
